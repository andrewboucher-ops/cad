Themes

Themes change the chrome only. Priority colours — the thing you triage on — are the same for every operator on every theme.

Using Harbour
The priority colours are not themeable.

P1–P4 (RED/AMBER/GREEN/ROUTINE), and every status dot, are fixed for every operator, in both light and dark. Priority colour is the loudest thing on screen and the accent is furniture — a console where an alarm colour could be set to pale blue is one where a real alarm could be made to look like a test signal. The colour-blind-safe ramp below is the one exception, and it is a different fixed ramp, not a colour picker.
