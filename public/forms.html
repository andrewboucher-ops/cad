You see the reports you filed, and any others your role or a named grant entitles you to. Restricted reports you are not named on are not listed — not even as hidden rows.

As an admin you can review every standard report here. Restricted reports (safeguarding, patient care) only appear once you are named as a reader of that form in Admin → Forms — that is a logged decision, not part of the admin role. Actioning a report takes it out of the open queue and tells whoever filed it; the report itself is kept. Edit the forms themselves.
