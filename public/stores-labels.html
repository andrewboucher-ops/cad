Stores labels

Back to stores

Tick what to print. Sheets are laid out for 21-to-a-page A4 labels (63.5 × 38.1 mm, e.g. Avery L7160) — print at 100% / "actual size". Every label is a QR code, which your 2D scanner and a phone camera both read.

Locations

Stick on a bag, box, shelf or vehicle locker. Scan an item then this label to move stock here, or to put an asset away here.

Goods in

One for the store room's delivery point. Scan an item then this label to receive a delivery (batch and expiry are asked for).

Stock items (drawer labels)

For drawers and bins. Not needed where the pack's own barcode is already linked to the item.

Assets

The asset's tag as a QR code, for kit that has no tag label yet.
