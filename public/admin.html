Reports from a restricted form can be read only by the officer who filed them and by the people named on that form below — not by any role, including this one. Adding or removing a name is logged. Open reports & review queue

The public job application form is the one filed against "Public job application" — anyone can fill it in at /apply.html without logging in, and each one arrives under Applicants. It must keep its required full_name and email fields and can't have photo fields; retire it to close applications.

Choose which sections each role sees — in the side menu on a desktop, and in the burger menu and home-screen buttons on a phone or tablet. A box you can't tick is a page that role can't use at all. Hiding a section takes it out of the menus; the page's own permissions still apply. Active users shows phone numbers, so it is checked on the server too.

A branch scopes what supervisors and field/MDT users see — dispatchers and admins always see every branch. A site, person, vehicle or asset with no branch set is shared and visible to everyone.

A course with an assessment is taken in-app from the officer terminal, scored server-side. A course without one is logged — by an admin, from a person's own record — after it happens elsewhere (a classroom session, a toolbox talk).

What a shift's type is, for rota colour-coding and the type-specific detail a shift carries (console/radio channel, patrol route, etc.). Retiring a type hides it from new shifts without touching ones that already use it.
